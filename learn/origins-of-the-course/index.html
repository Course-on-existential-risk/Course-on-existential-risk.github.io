About the course

Origins of the Course

Professor Stephen H. Shore originally designed and led this course for undergraduate students at Georgia State University, where it continues to be taught. The course’s original design and academic leadership are Professor Shore’s.

Abbad Ali and Suyog Thengale later helped develop the online course and contributed to selected modules. Abbad worked primarily on artificial intelligence and humanity’s future and subsequently prepared the free public edition.

Professor Shore retains copyright to the original course materials. The public edition brings the study of existential risk to learners beyond traditional university walls, with assignments and grading removed.
