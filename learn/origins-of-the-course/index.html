Origins of the Course

This course originated as an in-person Georgia State University course led and designed by Professor Stephen H. Shore for undergraduate students. The course was later brought online jointly with Abbad Ali and Suyog Thengale.

Professor Shore retains the copyright to the course material. Abbad and Suyog contributed to the online co-design, and Abbad prepared this public version.

Assignments, grading, and classroom administration were removed so the teaching materials could become a free public resource for learners beyond traditional university walls.

The public site preserves the course’s educational purpose while making the material easier to read independently.
