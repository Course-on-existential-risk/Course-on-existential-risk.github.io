Course Overview

Existential Risk: An Open Course introduces the tools and ideas people use to understand risks that could affect humanity’s future.

Course structure

The course moves from everyday risk and risk management to existential risk, ethics, coordination, specific threats, artificial intelligence, and humanity’s future.

  • Module 1: Measuring and managing risks
  • Module 2: Assessing existential risks
  • Module 3: Specific existential risks
  • Module 4: AI Risk
  • Module 5: Conclusion and Humanity’s Future

Required materials

You need a copy of Toby Ord’s book, The Precipice, and the ability to click links to readings, videos, and other sources.

This public edition is self-paced and has no account, grading, or assignment submission system.
