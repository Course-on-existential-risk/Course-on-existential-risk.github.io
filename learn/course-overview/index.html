Course guide

Start Here

Your guide to beginning the course.

Read this guide, explore the syllabus, and review how to navigate. Then continue to Welcome to Module 1.

The course arc

Move from everyday decisions to risks that affect humanity as a whole, examine specific threats, and finish by considering the future we want to protect.

  1. Measuring and Managing Risks: How do we measure and manage risk?
  2. Assessing Existential Risks: How should we assess risks to humanity?
  3. Specific Existential Risks: Which specific threats could endanger our future?
  4. AI Risk: What risks could advanced AI create, and how can we manage them?
  5. Conclusion and Humanity’s Future: What future are we trying to protect?

Required materials

Required textbook: a copy of Toby Ord’s The Precipice.

You also need an internet connection and the ability to click links to readings, videos, and audio.

The Reference Shelf contains 179 people and sources for further exploration. These optional reference links are separate from the required textbook.

How to study

Follow the modules in order, read the explanations, use the linked materials, and pause to reflect on the questions. There are no public deadlines, graded submissions, or accounts.
